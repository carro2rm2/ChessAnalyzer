How to interpret this

Floor: The 20th percentile of rolling performance ratings. It represents a lower typical stretch, not a guaranteed minimum.

Typical: The median rolling performance rating.

Ceiling: The 80th percentile of rolling performance ratings. It represents a stronger typical stretch.

Performance trend: The average of the five most recent rolling performance ratings minus the average of the first five. A positive number means recent results were stronger than earlier results.

Best stretch: The strongest individual window found in the games analyzed. A short hot streak is less reliable than a longer sample.

The trend is a comparison of two periods, not an official Chess.com rating change or a guarantee of future results.
